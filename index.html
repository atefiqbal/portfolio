01 / Campaign

Heartland
Antlers

Pet · D2C · Meta Ads

The brief: A premium elk/deer antler chew brand in a crowded pet market dominated by cheap rawhide and "natural" buzzwords. Most competitors talk about ingredients. We talked about behavior.

Every hook was built around a trigger event — the moment a dog owner types "why does my dog chew everything" into Google. The creative moved people from problem-aware to solution-aware without ever opening with the product.

Problem Hook
One chew, fewer interruptions — Functional benefit headline. Targets owners mid-workday.
Curiosity Hook
Why your dog watches your work — Trigger-event copy. Hooks a working dog owner's identity.
Authority Hook
Before you buy an antler — Educational angle. Pre-empts objections from first-time buyers.
Emotional Hook
Give your dog a job — Identity-based creative targeting working-breed owners.
Objection
Dental chews don't clean teeth — Contrarian hook. Creates pattern interrupt for pet owners.
Comparison
Brown vs. white antler — Side-by-side education. Raises perceived expertise of the buyer.
Life-Stage
Puppy teeth are no joke — Life-stage targeting. New puppy owners at peak anxiety.
Ingredient
What's in your dog's chew right now? — Ingredient skeptic angle. Mirrors Lisa-style persona.
Brand Story
Why we refuse to sell cheap dog chews — Brand values creative. Builds trust through transparency.
Product creative — Antler marrow. Lifestyle/Pinterest format.
Product creative — Antler marrow variant. Visual A/B test.
02 / Campaign

YOURS
Non-Alcoholic Wine

Beverage · Lifestyle · Email + Social

The brief: A premium NA wine brand entering a category still fighting the "why bother" objection. The creative challenge: make non-alcoholic wine feel like a choice you're proud of, not a compromise you settled for.

Strategy pivot: stop trying to out-wine wine. Position around the morning after — waking up sharp, remembering your conversations, keeping your plans. The alcohol-free angle becomes an advantage, not an asterisk.

Chardonnay Without The Morning-After — Full email creative. Positions freedom as the premium.
Red Blend — Product creative. Social/ad format.
Sauvignon Blanc — Product creative variant.
Lifestyle creative — Social/feed format.
Lifestyle variant — A/B test creative.
Rich reds, crisp whites — Announcement/seasonal creative.
03 / Campaign

Beauty &
Skincare

GLY Miracle · Restora · Kisa

The brief: Multiple beauty and skincare brands needing creative that doesn't look like everyone else's. The category is saturated with before/afters and dermatologist authority hooks — both lanes are overcrowded.

Approach: find the white space. GLY Miracle creative focuses on the bold, confident woman who already knows her skin — not the anxious shopper looking for a fix. Restora and Kisa lean into lifestyle identity over ingredient lists.

Hero Creative
GLY Miracle — Bold portrait creative. Confidence-first positioning, not anxiety-driven.
GLY Miracle — Main product image. Premium lifestyle aesthetic.
Restora — Product/lifestyle creative. Clean, editorial aesthetic.
Kisa by Pyropet — Lifestyle brand creative. Dark aesthetic, mood-forward approach.

Step 01
Saturation Scan
Pull active competitor ads from Meta Ad Library. Identify the lane 80% of competitors are crowding — then stay out of it.
Step 02
Trigger Mapping
List the moments that make someone search your category at 11pm. Almost never "I want a product." Almost always a life event, a photo, a frustration.
Step 03
White Space Test
For every angle candidate: would a media buyer at your top competitor recognize this angle? If yes, kill it. If no, brief it.
Step 04
Hook Variants
Every concept ships with 3 hook variants — problem, curiosity, and identity. The algorithm picks the winner. We pick the direction.
