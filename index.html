05 / Method

How I
Write Hooks

Framework · Process

Every hook starts with the trigger event — the specific moment a person reaches for their phone and searches. Not the demographic. Not the product benefit. The moment.

Then I identify which of five hook types creates the most tension between where they are and where they want to be.

Step 01
Find the Trigger Event
The specific moment — midnight search, frustrating product failure, sudden realization — that sends someone looking for a solution.
Step 02
Map Awareness Level
Unaware → Problem-aware → Solution-aware → Product-aware. Hook type changes based on where the audience is in the journey.
Step 03
Test 5 Hook Types
Problem, Curiosity, Reframe, Identity, Data. Each targets a different psychological lever. Always test all five before scaling.
Step 04
Scale the Winner
One winning hook becomes a full creative suite — static, video, email, UGC brief — built around the same emotional core.
